### Executive Overview

#### 1. System Architecture & Workflows
- **Offline-First & Serverless**: Runs 100% offline in browser `LocalStorage` with zero backend server dependencies.
- **Angular 20 Signals Reactivity**: Built with top-down single-direction signals (`computed()`). Adding a delivery, payment, expense, or missing can instantly updates all customer ledgers, KPI cards, and trend charts without reloading.
- **Multi-Sheet ExcelJS Integration**: Synchronizes 8 workbook sheets (`Dashboard`, `Customers`, `Daily Deliveries`, `Expenses`, `Profit`, `Missing Cans`, `Payments`, `Settings`) seamlessly.

---

#### 2. Quick Start Guide for New Users

1. **Sign In**:
   - **Username**: `admin` | **Password**: `admin123`
2. **First-Time Setup (Settings)**:
   - Configure **Company Name**, **Standard Can Price** (e.g., `₹40`), **Deposit Amount** (e.g., `₹500`), and **Light/Dark Mode**.
3. **Register Customers (Customers)**:
   - Add customer details, mobile number, delivery area, and security deposit.
4. **Log Daily Deliveries (Deliveries)**:
   - Select customer, enter filled cans delivered and empty cans returned. Rate, total amount ($=\text{filledCans} \times \text{rate}$), and balance ($=\text{total} - \text{paid}$) auto-calculate automatically. Click **Print Invoice** for customer receipts.
5. **Record Customer Payments (Payments)**:
   - Record cash/UPI payments against customer ledger accounts to reduce outstanding balances.
6. **Track Business Expenses (Expenses)**:
   - Log daily operating costs across Fuel, Salary, Maintenance, Electricity, Vehicle, or Miscellaneous categories.
7. **Manage Missing / Lost Cans (Missing Cans)**:
   - Record unreturned cans. Lost quantity ($=\text{missing} - \text{recovered}$) and replacement loss amounts update automatically.
8. **Export Reports & Backups (Reports & Settings)**:
   - View daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly profitability reports. Click **Export Excel** or **Export Backup** to download `.xlsx` spreadsheets for complete offline backup and multi-device transfer.
